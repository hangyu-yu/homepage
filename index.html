Energy materials & systems

Hangyu Yu

Postdoctoral researcher · Group of Energy Materials

École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), Switzerland

I work on solid oxide cell technology — fuel cells and electrolysers — with a focus on what happens to them while they run. My PhD at EPFL (2021–2025) covered operando diagnosis, optimization and degradation of these devices; I stayed on as a postdoc in 2025.

Most of my work sits between the electrochemistry and the system: impedance spectroscopy and segmented in-situ characterization on one side, real-time optimization and techno-economic analysis of the surrounding energy system on the other. I also develop EISAP, a software tool for electrochemical impedance spectroscopy analysis.
